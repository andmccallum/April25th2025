
Title: Novel Approaches to Non-Destructive Magnetic Field Measurement in Field-Reversed Configuration (FRC)
=================================================================================================

1. Current Limitations Analysis
-----------------------------
The challenge of direct magnetic field measurement inside FRC plasmas stems from:
a) Extreme plasma conditions (T > 10⁶ K)
b) High magnetic field gradients
c) Limited diagnostic access
d) Temporal resolution requirements (μs scale)
e) Spatial resolution requirements (mm scale)

Current methods (Faraday rotation, THz polarimetry) are limited by:
- Signal attenuation in dense plasma
- Temporal resolution constraints
- Integration effects along measurement paths



2. Quantum-Enhanced Measurement Framework
-------------------------------------
2.1 Quantum State Evolution Operator:
Ĥ(B) = -μ·B + (ℏ²/2m)∇² + V(r)

where:
- μ: magnetic moment operator
- B: magnetic field vector
- V(r): confining potential

2.2 Quantum Measurement Operator:
M̂(B) = ∑ᵢ cᵢ|ψᵢ⟩⟨ψᵢ|

where:
- |ψᵢ⟩: quantum measurement basis states
- cᵢ: measurement coefficients



3. Proposed Measurement Solutions
------------------------------
3.1 Quantum-Enhanced Polarimetry (QEP)
-------------------------------------
Utilizing quantum entangled photon pairs:
ρ(B) = |Ψ⟩⟨Ψ| = (1/√2)(|H₁V₂⟩ + e^(iφ(B))|V₁H₂⟩)

Measurement sensitivity:
ΔB = ℏ/(μₐτ√N)

where:
- φ(B): B-field dependent phase
- μₐ: effective magnetic moment
- τ: measurement time
- N: number of entangled photon pairs

3.2 Topological Phase Detection (TPD)
----------------------------------
Based on Berry phase accumulation:
γ(B) = i∮⟨ψ(R)|∇ᵣψ(R)⟩·dR

Measurement precision:
δB = 2π/(gμₐT)

where:
- g: gyromagnetic ratio
- T: evolution time

3.3 Quantum Tunneling Magnetometry (QTM)
-------------------------------------
Using quantum tunneling through magnetic barriers:
T(B) = exp(-2∫√(2m(V(x,B)-E)/ℏ²)dx)

Sensitivity scaling:
ΔB ∝ 1/√(τₜN)

where:
- τₜ: tunneling time
- N: number of tunneling events
