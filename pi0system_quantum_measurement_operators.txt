Pi0System Quantum Measurement Operators
====================================

Quantum_State_Evolution:
Symbol: Û
Equation: Û(t) = exp(-iĤt/ℏ)
Description: Evolves quantum states in magnetic field

Entanglement_Generator:
Symbol: Ê
Equation: |Ψₑ⟩ = Ê|Ψ₁⟩|Ψ₂⟩ = (1/√2)(|0₁1₂⟩ + |1₁0₂⟩)
Description: Generates entangled states for measurement

Phase_Evolution:
Symbol: Φ̂
Equation: Φ̂(B) = exp(iγ∮A·dl + i∫B·dS)
Description: Tracks phase evolution in magnetic field

Measurement_Projection:
Symbol: P̂
Equation: P̂(B) = ∑ᵢ λᵢ|ϕᵢ(B)⟩⟨ϕᵢ(B)|
Description: Projects quantum states for measurement

Error_Correction:
Symbol: Ê
Equation: E(ρ) = ∑ᵢ EᵢρEᵢ†
Description: Corrects quantum measurement errors
